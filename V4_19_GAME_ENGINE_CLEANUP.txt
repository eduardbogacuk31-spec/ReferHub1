ReferHub v4.19 — Game Engine Cleanup

ROOT CAUSE FIXED:
- game-v2.js and game-pro.js were both still loaded.
- Both files defined their own global openGameDetail().
- They also contained their own post-game setTimeout(() => openGameDetail(...)).
- Because they loaded after app.js, they overrode the current Game Center and bypassed v4.18 Live Cooldown.

CHANGES:
- Removed game-v2.js from index.html.
- Removed game-pro.js from index.html.
- Kept current app.js Game Center as the single active engine.
- Kept v4.17 Game Experience.
- Kept v4.18 Live Cooldown.
- Added final single-engine guard to prevent a later legacy override.

EXPECTED RESULT:
- Completing a round no longer reloads the full game screen.
- Scroll position remains where the player is.
- Live cooldown counts down in place.
- At 00:00 controls unlock automatically.
- Player can immediately play another round.

Old JS files remain in the archive only as unused legacy files; they are no longer loaded by the app.
