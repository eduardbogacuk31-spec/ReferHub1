ReferHub v4.26 — Seasons + Streaks

NEW:
- Dedicated Season tab.
- Season I: Genesis Season.
- 30-level season progression.
- Season XP calculated from existing real account/referral data.
- Referrals are the dominant XP source: +500 seasonal XP each.
- Active referrals: +180 seasonal XP each.
- 25% of account XP contributes to season score.
- Live progress to next level.
- Season countdown.
- Season reward path with cosmetic/title concepts.
- Referral Boost card linking directly to Referral Hub.
- Streak UI foundation.

IMPORTANT:
- No fake login history was created.
- Current backend does not expose a historical daily-login ledger, so v4.26
  only shows a safe current streak indicator.
- Reward cards are progression/cosmetic UI and do not mutate RH balance.
- Existing referral system, games, admin and economy are preserved.
